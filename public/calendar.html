📅 Subscribe from your own calendar

Add this feed once in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook and every new or updated event shows up automatically — no need to check back.

Add to my calendar app
Instructions per app

Google Calendar (desktop): Other calendars → + → "From URL" → paste the copied link → Add calendar.

Apple Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription → paste the link → Subscribe. Or just tap "Add to my calendar app" on an iPhone/Mac.

Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste the link.
